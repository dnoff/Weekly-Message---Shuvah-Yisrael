## Message Outline

*Yeshua's Prayer — John 17:1–26 (Part 3)*  
Rabbi Larry Feldman

**Main Idea:** Believers should be confident and assured because Yeshua prays for our protection, sanctification, and glory.

### Yeshua Intercedes for Us

**Romans 8:26–27, 34** — The Spirit intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words; and Messiah Yeshua, who was raised and is at the right hand of God, also intercedes for us.

**1 John 2:1** — If anyone sins, we have an Advocate with the Father, Yeshua Messiah the righteous.

**Hebrews 7:25** — He is able to save forever those who draw near to God through Him, since He always lives to make intercession for them.

**Hebrews 9:24** — Messiah entered heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us.

### I. Yeshua prays for His own ultimate glorification

#### A. Through His authority

**John 17:1–3**  
Yeshua lifted up His eyes to heaven and said, “Father, the hour has come; glorify Your Son, that the Son may glorify You, even as You gave Him authority over all flesh, that to all whom You have given Him, He may give eternal life. This is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Yeshua Messiah whom You have sent.”

Supporting Scriptures: Matthew 28:18–19 (all authority given); Psalm 2:4–9 (the installed King); John 5:27–29 (authority to judge); John 3:16–18, 36 (belief and life); John 6:28–29 (the work of God is to believe).

#### B. Through His works

**John 17:4–5**  
“I glorified You on the earth, having accomplished the work which You have given Me to do. Now, Father, glorify Me together with Yourself, with the glory which I had with You before the world was.”

Supporting Scripture: Philippians 2:5–11 (Yeshua humbled Himself, therefore God highly exalted Him).

### II. Yeshua prays for His disciples' protection and sanctification

#### A. For the disciples' protection and joy

**John 17:6–16**  
Yeshua manifested the Father's name to the men given to Him; He asks the Father to keep them in His name, that they may be one, guarded from the evil one, with His joy made full in themselves — for they are not of the world, even as He is not of the world.

Supporting Scriptures: Luke 6:12–16 (choosing the twelve); John 6:35, 37–40 (the bread of life); John 6:51–71 (many turn away; Peter confesses); John 8:24, 58 (“before Abraham was born, I am”); 1 John 2:15–17 (do not love the world); 1 John 5:19 (the world lies in the power of the evil one); Revelation 12:10 (the accuser thrown down); Job 1:6–11; 2:1–5 (Satan before the Lord).

#### B. For the disciples' sanctification

**John 17:17–19**  
“Sanctify them in the truth; Your word is truth. As You sent Me into the world, I also have sent them into the world. For their sakes I sanctify Myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.”

Supporting Scriptures: Matthew 28:18–20 (the Great Commission); 2 Corinthians 5:18–20 (the ministry of reconciliation; ambassadors for Messiah).

### III. Yeshua prays for His believers' unity and glorification

#### A. For the believers' unity

**John 17:20–23**  
“I do not ask on behalf of these alone, but for those also who believe in Me through their word; that they may all be one… so that the world may believe that You sent Me. The glory which You have given Me I have given to them, that they may be one, just as We are one.”

Supporting Scriptures: 1 Corinthians 12:12–13 (one body, one Spirit); John 10:15–16 (one flock, one shepherd); Ephesians 2:14, 19 (He is our peace, fellow citizens); Philippians 2:1–5 (united in spirit and purpose).

#### B. For the believers' glorification

**John 17:24–26**  
“Father, I desire that they also, whom You have given Me, be with Me where I am, so that they may see My glory… and I have made Your name known to them, and will make it known, so that the love with which You loved Me may be in them, and I in them.”

Supporting Scriptures: John 14:3 (I will come again and receive you); Colossians 3:1–4 (your life is hidden with Messiah); 1 John 3:1–2 (we will be like Him); 2 Corinthians 3:18 (transformed from glory to glory).

---

### Conclusion

Believers should be confident and assured because Yeshua prays for our **protection**, **sanctification**, and **glory**.
